THE PROBLEM

“The report is wrong.”
Now comes the detective work.

Finance, sales and operations teams raise the ticket. Data teams spend hours tracing the number by hand: from a visual, through a measure and a transformation, back to a job or a source record.

DIA brings that investigation into one evidence-backed explanation.

HOW IT WORKS

From question to cause.

A clear explanation for the user.
A complete handoff for the engineer.

  1. 01 /

    Understands the question

    Starts with a form, chat or ticket to understand the number being disputed.

  2. 02 /

    Traces the number

    Follows its path through the lineage graph, from report to source.

  3. 03 /

    Checks each layer

    Runs read-only checks to find where the evidence diverges.

  4. 04 /

    Explains with evidence

    Gives the cause in plain words, plus an engineer handoff pack with every query it ran.
